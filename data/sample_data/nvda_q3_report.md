# NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) - Q3 Fiscal 2025 Financial Results & Executive Review

## Executive Summary
NVIDIA Corporation reported record revenue for the third quarter ended October 27, 2024, of $35.08 billion, up 17% sequentially and up 94% from a year ago. Diluted GAAP earnings per share was $0.78, up 111% year-over-year.

---

## Data Center Demand & Hyperscaler Expansion
Data Center revenue was a record $30.77 billion, up 17% from the previous quarter and up 112% from a year ago.

**Management Commentary on Data Center Demand**:
"The age of AI is in full steam, propelling a global shift to NVIDIA accelerated computing," said Jensen Huang, founder and CEO of NVIDIA. "Demand for Hopper and anticipation for Blackwell — in full production — are incredible as foundation model makers scale pre-training, post-training and inference."

CFO Colette Kress added: "Data Center compute revenue grew 112% year-over-year driven by intense hyperscaler demand for the NVIDIA Hopper GPU computing platform. Cloud service providers represented approximately half of our Data Center sales. Sovereign AI demand also continues to expand across European and Asian governments building localized compute clusters."

---

## Blackwell Architecture Ramp
NVIDIA confirmed that Blackwell production is ramping up rapidly:
"Blackwell production is in full gear. We delivered 13,000 GPU samples to customers in the third quarter, including initial engineering systems to OpenAI," noted Jensen Huang. "Demand for Blackwell is staggering, and we expect demand to exceed supply for several quarters in fiscal 2026."

---

## Profitability, Gross Margins, and Outlook
- **Gross Margin**: GAAP gross margin was 74.6%, reflecting strong data center mix and operational efficiency.
- **Operating Income**: GAAP operating income reached $21.87 billion, up 110% year-over-year.
- **Q4 Outlook**: Revenue is expected to be $37.5 billion, plus or minus 2%, with gross margins remaining in the mid-70% range.
