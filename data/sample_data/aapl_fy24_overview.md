# Apple Inc. (AAPL) - Fiscal Year 2024 Financial Overview & Strategy Review

## Annual Highlights & Revenue
Apple Inc. posted total revenue of $391.04 billion for fiscal year 2024. For the fourth quarter ended September 28, 2024, revenue was $94.93 billion, an increase of 6% year-over-year.

---

## iPhone Performance & Apple Intelligence Momentum
iPhone revenue reached $46.22 billion in Q4, up 6% year-over-year.

CEO Tim Cook remarked: "During the quarter, we were thrilled to announce our best products yet, including the iPhone 16 lineup and Apple Intelligence, our personal intelligence system that ushers in a new era for our products and marks an inflection point in user experience."

---

## Services Segment All-Time High
Services revenue reached an all-time record of $24.97 billion in Q4, growing 12% year-over-year.
- Paid subscriptions across the platform surpassed 1 billion accounts.
- High gross margins in the Services business (~74%) continue to support company-wide gross margin expansion (reaching 46.2% in Q4).

---

## Capital Return and Cash Position
CFO Luca Maestri reported: "Our active installed base of devices has reached a new all-time high across all products and geographic segments. During the quarter, we returned nearly $29 billion to shareholders via share repurchases and dividends."
