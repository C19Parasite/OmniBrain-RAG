# Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) - Cloud & Enterprise AI Operational Review

## Financial Performance Overview
Microsoft Corp. reported quarterly revenue of $65.59 billion for Q1 FY25, an increase of 16% compared to the corresponding period of the prior fiscal year. Diluted earnings per share was $3.30, up 10% year-over-year.

---

## Microsoft Cloud & Azure AI Acceleration
Microsoft Cloud revenue was $38.9 billion, up 22% year-over-year, driven by continued demand for enterprise cloud and AI infrastructure.

CEO Satya Nadella commented: "AI-driven transformation is changing work, artifacts, and workflows across every role, function, and business process. We are expanding our data center footprint across four continents to support unprecedented customer demand for Azure AI models and compute clusters."

---

## Copilot Adoption and Enterprise Solutions
- Azure revenue growth was 33%, with 12 percentage points of growth directly attributable to AI services.
- Over 70% of Fortune 500 companies now deploy Microsoft 365 Copilot to streamline business operations.
- GitHub Copilot customer base grew over 55% year-over-year to 1.8 million paid subscribers.
